Published Work · Islamic Scholarship

Al-ʿIlm — العِلم
Etiquettes, Methods & Ways to Seek Knowledge
An authored work rooted in Islamic tradition, exploring the sacred dimensions of seeking knowledge (ṭalab al-ʿilm). Drawing from classical scholars and the Sunnah, this book guides the sincere seeker on how to orient their heart, mind, and conduct in the pursuit of beneficial knowledge.

It addresses the inner states, proper manners, and time-tested methods that distinguish true learning from mere information consumption — a work for the student of deen and dunya alike.

II Purifying the Intention (Niyyah)
III Etiquettes of the Student of Knowledge
IV Methods & Disciplines of Seeking ʿIlm
V Preserving, Acting on & Spreading Knowledge

The Inner World

The mind that wonders
never stops growing.

Most people separate their professional curiosity from their existential one. I refuse to. The same mind that dives into AI systems and blockchain infrastructure also asks deep questions about faith, character, and how humans truly grow.

What led me to write Al-ʿIlm wasn't a course or a degree — it was three things I couldn't ignore:

01
My students, whose relentless curiosity reminded me that the pursuit of knowledge is never truly finished — and that someone needed to map the path properly.
02
The youth today — brilliant, connected, yet unaware of the sacred etiquettes and methods of seeking knowledge, and blind to its true weight and importance.
03
The ignorance I saw around me — not lack of intelligence, but lack of direction. People drowning in information while starving for wisdom.
