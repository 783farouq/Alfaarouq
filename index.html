What led me to write Al-ʿIlm
1.
My students, whose relentless curiosity reminded me that the pursuit of knowledge is never truly finished — and that someone needed to map the path.
2.
The youth today — brilliant and connected, yet unaware of the sacred etiquettes of seeking knowledge and blind to its true weight and importance.
3.
The ignorance I saw around me — not a lack of intelligence, but a lack of direction. People drowning in information while starving for wisdom.
