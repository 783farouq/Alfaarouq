Al-ʿIlm

Book, 2025

A scholarly work on the etiquettes, methods and ways of seeking knowledge. It draws on the Sunnah and classical sources, including al-Zarnuji's Taʿlīm al-Mutaʿallim, and carries Arabic poems with English translations.

العِلم Al-ʿIlm Knowledge, and the etiquettes of seeking it

Chapters include

  1. The Virtue and Obligation of Knowledge in Islam
  2. Purifying the Intention (Niyyah)
  3. Etiquettes of the Student of Knowledge
  4. Methods and Disciplines of Seeking ʿIlm
  5. Preserving, Acting on and Spreading Knowledge

What led me to write Al-ʿIlm

  1. My students. Their curiosity reminded me that the pursuit of knowledge is never finished, and that someone needed to map the path.
  2. The youth today. Brilliant and connected, yet often unaware of the etiquettes of seeking knowledge and its true weight.
  3. The ignorance I saw around me. Not a lack of intelligence but a lack of direction: people drowning in information while starving for wisdom.
